Privacy Policy for ScrollCount

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

ScrollCount ("we", "our", or "us") provides the ScrollCount mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and device data.

1. Information We Collect

ScrollCount is built with privacy-first principles:

  • Usage and Scroll Metrics: All metrics, scroll measurements, daily usage thresholds, and app statistics calculated by ScrollCount are processed strictly on your local device. We do not transmit, sell, or store your usage statistics on external servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, phone number, or contacts).

2. App Permissions & Services

To measure scroll actions and provide mindful usage alerts, the App may require standard system or accessibility permissions solely to detect scroll interactions locally. These permissions are strictly utilized for foreground/background metric counters and are never used to log keystrokes, personal messages, or private screen content.

3. Third-Party Services & Billing

If you purchase subscriptions or in-app features, payment transactions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing via Google Play Services. We do not store or have access to your credit card or financial details. Google's privacy policy applies to payment data handled during purchases.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because your usage records remain stored locally on your device, deleting the app or clearing its application cache permanently removes all stored data from your device.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
